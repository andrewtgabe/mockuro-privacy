Delete Your Account and Data

MockUro, developed by Andrew T. Gabrielson, MD

You can delete your MockUro account and its data at any time, either in the app or by email. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it first in the App Store or Google Play so you are not charged again.

Option 1: Delete in the app

Open MockUro and go to Account → Profile → Delete Account, then confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the email address on your MockUro account with the subject line "Delete my account":

mockuroapp@gmail.com

We will confirm by email and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

• Account information: your name, email address, and institution
• Study data: completed case history and scores, spaced repetition records, marked cases, achievements, and case feedback you submitted
• Device records: the device identifiers used to enforce the two-device limit

What is kept

• Anonymized statistics: aggregated usage data that cannot be linked to you may be kept indefinitely to improve the app
• Purchase records: Apple and Google keep their own records of your subscription purchases under their policies; we cannot delete those

More information

See the MockUro Privacy Policy for full details on the data we collect and how it is used.
